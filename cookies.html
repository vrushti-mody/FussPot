Cookie policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page describes the browser storage used by this informational website.

At launch, this site does not load an analytics service. Optional analytics consent is included so a future analytics tool can only be enabled after a visitor opts in; the tool must not be activated until the site owner configures and documents it.

Essential local storage

The site uses browser local storage to remember your choice for essential or optional measurement. This is used to keep the privacy banner from reappearing on every page. This value is stored on your device and is not used to identify you.

Optional analytics

Analytics are off by default. If analytics are added, the site should explain the provider, the information collected, how long it is retained, and any transfers before seeking consent. You can reject optional analytics or update your decision through the “Privacy choices” control in the footer.

Third-party map

The homepage embeds a Google map. Loading the embedded map may allow Google to use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies. To avoid loading the embedded map, use the direct directions link instead. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Changing your choice

Open “Privacy choices” at the bottom of any page to change your selection. You can also clear local storage for this site using your browser settings; the site will then ask again.
