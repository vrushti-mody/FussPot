Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how the Fuss Pot informational website handles information when you visit. The site is designed to provide public information about the boutique, its location and ways to get in touch. It does not take orders or payments.

Before launch, replace or confirm the business contact details and have this notice reviewed for the jurisdictions where the site and business operate. This page is general website information, not legal advice.

Who is responsible?

The site is presented for Fuss Pot, a fashion boutique in Khar West, Mumbai, India. A business contact for privacy requests has not been published here because a verified privacy contact was not available. For general enquiries, contact the store at +91 98190 85507 or via Instagram.

Information processed

This static site does not request account details, payment details, or contact-form submissions. Your browser may send standard technical information to the hosting provider when loading the site, such as IP address, device and browser information, requested pages, and timestamps, for delivery, security, and troubleshooting.

The embedded map is served by Google Maps. Opening it may cause Google to receive technical information and apply its own privacy practices. You can instead use the “Open in Google Maps” link. Instagram and telephone links also take you to services outside this site.

Cookies and local storage

Essential local storage remembers your privacy selection. Optional analytics are off by default and no analytics library is included in this site build. If analytics are added later, they must remain disabled until consent where required, and this notice and the cookie notice must be updated before activation.

Your choices and rights

You can reject optional analytics or revisit your choice using “Privacy choices” in the footer. Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information, or to object to its processing. Contact the business through the public channels above to make a request; identity verification may be needed before responding. California residents may also have rights concerning the sale or sharing of personal information. This site does not sell personal information and does not include cross-context advertising technology.

Children

This informational website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may revise this notice when the website or its data practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
